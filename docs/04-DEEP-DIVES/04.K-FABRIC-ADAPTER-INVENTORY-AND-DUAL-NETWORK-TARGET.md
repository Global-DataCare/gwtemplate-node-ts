# Fabric and Pontus-X responsibility boundary

Status: Historical filename retained for link compatibility

This document previously described a target direct Pontus-X adapter inside GW
CORE. That target is no longer the canonical architecture.

## Current decision

- Hyperledger Fabric is the permissioned ledger used by GW CORE for the
  governed state and evidence assigned to that network.
- The organization controller publishes products, datasets and service
  offerings in Pontus-X through the `globaldatacare.es` portal and its BFF.
- Pontus-X operational credentials, publication state and user-facing product
  workflow belong to the portal/BFF, not to GW CORE or dataspace ICA.
- GW CORE exposes the protected FHIR and digital-twin data-plane services that
  a published offering may reference.
- Dataspace ICA verifies participants and representatives and exposes technical
  trust/discovery information. Its optional metadata synchronization is not the
  controller's Pontus-X publication flow.

GW CORE therefore does not need a direct Pontus-X ledger adapter to satisfy
this architecture. The existing `fabric`, `mem` and multi-adapter behavior must
not be overloaded with portal-owned Pontus-X behavior.

## Discovery layers

1. ICA publishes trust and operator discovery information.
2. GW publishes host, tenant, DID, DSP and DCAT technical discovery surfaces.
3. The controller publishes organization products and offerings in Pontus-X
   through the portal/BFF.

These layers may reference the same organization or service, but they do not
have the same owner or purpose.
